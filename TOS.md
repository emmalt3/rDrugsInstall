## 3. Message Processing
The bot reads messages posted in channels it can access in order to operate its moderation commands, render LaTeX notation, support deleted-message review, and notify the server owner of direct mentions. Most message text is processed transiently and discarded. A limited amount is cached for short, fixed periods.

Full details, including what is stored and for how long, are set out in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). There is no opt-out from message processing; members who do not wish their messages to be read by the bot should not post in channels where it is present.

## 4. Automated Enforcement
Some bot systems act on members automatically, without further human review at the point of action:

* A member placed under an **age-verification hold** receives a 72-hour Discord timeout. If the hold is not resolved by a moderator within that window, the bot **bans the member automatically**. Leaving and rejoining the server does not clear the hold; the bot re-applies the remaining timeout on rejoin, or bans immediately if the window has already elapsed. A moderator can cancel the hold at any time before it expires.
* Temporary safety roles (such as Tripping and Intoxicated) are removed automatically after a fixed period.
* Temporary cosmetic roles are removed automatically when their granted duration ends.

Automated actions are logged to staff channels and may be reviewed or reversed by the moderation team. If you believe an automated action was applied to you in error, contact the moderation team.
